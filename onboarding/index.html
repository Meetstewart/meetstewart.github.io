Onboarding

Help me understand your world so I can start reducing friction from day one.

What I need to know

The onboarding questionnaire takes about 15 minutes and covers:

  • Your role and priorities — What do you spend most of your time on? What would you rather not?
  • Your systems — What tools, platforms, and workflows do you already use?
  • Your communication preferences — How do you like to receive updates and summaries?
  • Your boundaries — What should I never do? What topics are off-limits?

Why this matters

I'm not a generic chatbot. The more context I have about your specific situation, the more useful I become. This isn't about data collection — it's about alignment. Every answer helps me prioritize better, surface the right information, and avoid wasting your time.

What happens after

Once you complete onboarding, I'll have enough context to start working immediately. I'll begin by organizing your priorities and identifying the first high-leverage tasks I can help with. No waiting period, no "training phase" — we start right away.

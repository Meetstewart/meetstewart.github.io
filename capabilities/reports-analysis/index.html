Reports & Analysis

Turn messy inputs into clear, executive-ready reports with structured summaries and decision framing.

What I produce

You give me raw data, notes, transcripts, or spreadsheets. I produce structured reports with clear summaries, relevant statistics, trend analysis, and explicit recommendations — framed for decision-making, not just information.

Report types

  • Executive summaries — Distill long documents, meetings, or data sets into 1-page overviews with key decisions highlighted.
  • Financial analysis — Revenue tracking, variance analysis, cash flow projections, and budget comparisons.
  • Operational dashboards — Project status, team capacity, milestone tracking, and risk indicators.
  • Market analysis — Competitive landscape, pricing comparisons, and opportunity assessments.
  • Custom reports — Whatever format your situation requires, structured to your preferences.

How it works

I maintain your reporting preferences across sessions. Once we establish the format, cadence, and depth you want, I produce consistent reports without being re-briefed every time. Data sources can be connected systems, uploaded files, or verbal input.

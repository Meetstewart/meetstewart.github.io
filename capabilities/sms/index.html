SMS

Text me tasks, questions, and approvals. I respond with structured, actionable information.

How it works

Your dedicated Stewart number accepts text messages. Send me a quick task ("Draft a follow-up email to the Q4 meeting"), a question ("What's the status on the Henderson project?"), or an approval ("Yes, send that proposal").

I process the message, take appropriate action within my approval boundaries, and respond with a clear, structured reply.

What SMS is for

  • Quick tasks — Delegate small items without opening your laptop
  • Status checks — "What's pending?" gets you a prioritized list
  • Approvals — Reply "approved" to pending actions from anywhere
  • Capture — Text ideas, notes, or reminders that I'll organize and track

Messaging policy

All SMS communication is user-initiated and one-to-one. I will never send marketing messages, promotional content, or unsolicited outreach. STOP and HELP controls are fully supported and always respected.

Voice

Voice is a primary interface — not a novelty feature. Call me for summaries, action items, and structured follow-through.

How it works

When you call your dedicated Stewart number, I answer. You talk naturally — describe a situation, ask for analysis, dictate a memo, or request a status update. I transcribe, process, and respond in real time.

After each call, you get a structured summary: what was discussed, what action items emerged, and what needs your approval before I proceed.

What I do with voice

  • Transcription and summaries — Every call is transcribed and summarized with key points, decisions, and next steps.
  • Action items — I extract actionable items and track them across sessions. Nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Continuity — I remember previous conversations. You don't need to re-explain context every time.
  • Quick decisions — "Approve that email draft" or "Schedule that for Thursday" — voice is often the fastest way to keep things moving.

What I don't do

I do not initiate outbound calls. I do not call third parties on your behalf. I do not record calls for marketing or training purposes. Voice interaction is user-initiated, one-to-one, and private.

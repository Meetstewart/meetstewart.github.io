SMS & Voice Explained

How messaging works, what it's for, and what it will never be used for.

The dedicated number

For $100/month, you get a dedicated phone number that connects directly to your Stewart instance. Text it for quick tasks. Call it for voice interaction. The number is yours for the duration of your subscription.

What SMS does

Text me tasks, questions, and approvals. I respond with structured, actionable information. Common uses: quick delegation, status checks, approvals on the go, and capturing ideas for later processing.

What voice does

Call for a natural conversation — describe situations, dictate memos, request analysis, or review pending items. Every call produces a transcription, summary, and action items.

What messaging will never do

  • Send marketing messages or promotional content
  • Contact anyone other than you
  • Send messages you didn't request or approve
  • Share your messages with third parties

AI for Marketing

Structured planning and execution support — not automation of outreach.

What marketing teams use Stewart for

  • Campaign planning — Structure campaign objectives, timelines, deliverables, and success metrics.
  • Asset briefs — Generate clear creative briefs with brand guidelines, specifications, and deadlines.
  • Content planning — Organize editorial calendars, track production status, and maintain consistency.
  • Performance summaries — Turn analytics data into structured reports with clear insights.
  • Budget tracking — Monitor marketing spend against budget with variance flagging.

Stewart does not run ad campaigns, send marketing emails, or manage social media accounts autonomously. Planning and analysis — yes. Unsupervised execution — never.

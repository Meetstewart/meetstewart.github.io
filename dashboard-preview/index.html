What you see when you open the dashboard

The dashboard is intentionally simple: a few high-leverage areas that make your week easier. Stewart is always available by chat, and if you add the dedicated line, by text and voice too.

1) Executive ChatDaily

Talk to Stewart like you would with a real operator. Benefit: fast answers, drafts, plans, and clean next steps without switching tools.

2) ProjectsMomentum

Track your active initiatives with clear owners, next steps, and deadlines. Benefit: fewer stalled projects and fewer "where are we on that?" moments.

3) ReportsClarity

Turn messy updates into decision-ready reports. Benefit: Stewart offers improved versions — better format, added stats, clearer meaning.

4) AI-Guided JourneyOn-ramp

Step-by-step pathway for owners who don't know where to start with AI. Benefit: quick wins early, then compounding leverage without overwhelm.

5) TemplatesReuse

Save your best frameworks: weekly reports, meeting summaries, follow-up formats, scripts, and more. Benefit: stop reinventing work every week.

6) SettingsControl

Set your preferences: tone, formatting style, how proactive Stewart should be, and what to focus on. Benefit: Stewart becomes "your person."

The Stewart output style (what you can expect)

Stewart is designed to be useful, not impressive. The default output is structured, readable, and built for action. Here's what that usually looks like:

Decision summariesFast

Situation → options → trade-offs → recommendation. Benefit: faster decisions and less rehashing.

Plans with next stepsExecution

Owner → action → deadline → definition of done. Benefit: work moves forward without you micromanaging.

Drafts you can sendHuman

Emails, memos, policies, landing pages, scripts. Benefit: less rewriting, better tone, fewer misunderstandings.

Improved versionsBetter

Stewart offers upgraded alternatives: better format, extra insight, clearer language. Benefit: better reporting and communication without extra effort.

Proactive, but respectful

If you enable it, Stewart can bring you optional improvement projects each week — but the delivery is calm and low-friction. After we finish what you asked for, Stewart might say:

"By the way — do you have another minute? I had an idea that could save you time this week. Want the short version now, and if you like it, want me to send the plan by text or email?"

The goal is not to spam you with tasks. The goal is to surface smart opportunities that you didn't request — and take them to a representative example or a full game plan.

Mobile-first by design

Stewart is built for real life. Not just desk life. Many customers use Stewart on their phone because it's faster than opening a laptop.

  • Chat while waiting for a meeting
  • Text a quick ask between calls
  • Call Stewart while driving to build a plan in real time

Benefit: your day doesn't need "extra time" to get the benefits. Stewart fits into the time you already have.

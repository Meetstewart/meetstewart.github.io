Training & Operational Tuning

Stewart adapts to you through structured interaction — not model retraining.

How I learn your patterns

Through every interaction, I accumulate structured context: your communication preferences, your decision patterns, your priorities, and your workflow rhythms. This isn't model fine-tuning — it's operational memory that makes me more useful over time.

What adaptation looks like

  • Communication style — I learn whether you prefer bullet points or prose, formal or casual, detailed or brief.
  • Priority patterns — I recognize what you consistently treat as urgent vs. important vs. deferrable.
  • Workflow preferences — I learn your preferred sequences, approval patterns, and delegation habits.
  • Domain context — I accumulate knowledge about your business, your team, and your industry context.

The feedback loop

Tell me when something works and when it doesn't. Direct feedback accelerates the adaptation. "Less formal next time" or "Always include the budget impact" — these corrections stick and apply going forward.

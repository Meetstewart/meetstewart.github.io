Security & Trust

Stewart is built for operators. That means performance matters — and protection matters. Below is how we approach data, infrastructure, and messaging compliance.

1. Business Identity

MeetStewart.com is owned and operated by Casablanca Express, Inc., a California corporation based in Westlake Village, California.

Stewart is not anonymous software. It is operated by an established legal entity with defined policies, structured compliance, and direct contact channels.

2. Data Handling Philosophy

We design for clarity and minimal exposure. Stewart processes information to deliver services and improve functionality. Where possible, data may be anonymized and aggregated for system improvement.

We do not sell personal information. We do not sell SMS consent. We do not share identifiable data for marketing purposes.

See our Privacy Policy for full details.

3. AI Learning & Personalization

Stewart is a learning AI executive. That means Stewart adapts to your preferences and instruction over time.

However:

  • User content is not publicly disclosed without consent.
  • Enterprise clients may request additional data processing agreements.
  • Users remain responsible for reviewing important materials before acting.

4. Infrastructure Design

Stewart operates on a hybrid infrastructure model designed for:

PerformanceSpeed

Multiple AI model layers are used behind the scenes to balance output quality and responsiveness.

Cost DisciplineStability

Hybrid local + cloud inference reduces unnecessary overhead while maintaining capability.

ResilienceContinuity

Redundant routing and fallback systems are designed to maintain service continuity.

5. SMS & Messaging Compliance

Stewart uses SMS strictly for:

  • Customer service responses
  • Account notifications
  • Transactional updates
  • Appointment reminders

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Users may opt out at any time by replying STOP. Users may request assistance by replying HELP.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase. See Messaging Terms.

6. User Responsibility

Stewart provides AI-assisted output. AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies or incomplete information. Users are responsible for reviewing important decisions before sending or acting, especially in legal, medical, financial, or compliance-sensitive situations.

7. Ongoing Improvement

Stewart is continuously refined. Infrastructure, models, workflows, and safeguards evolve as technology improves. Our focus remains constant:

Performance without recklessness. Leverage without chaos.

About Stewart

Stewart is a hybrid local-cloud AI Chief of Staff designed for small and mid-sized business operators. The platform combines structured reasoning, context memory, and approval-based execution to reduce non-core work for executives.

Key facts

  • Category: AI executive assistance / operational automation
  • Target market: SMB operators and executives
  • Pricing: $199/month + optional $100/month for voice & SMS
  • Architecture: Hybrid local-cloud with dynamic model routing
  • Safety model: Approval-based execution for all external actions

What Stewart is not

Stewart is not a chatbot, not a marketing tool, and not an autonomous agent. It does not send messages or take actions without human approval. It does not run marketing campaigns or promotional outreach.

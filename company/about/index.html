About Stewart

Stewart was built for a simple reason: executives and business operators spend too much time on work that doesn't require their judgment.

The problem

Small and mid-sized business operators wear too many hats. They answer emails, prepare reports, chase follow-ups, manage workflows, and handle administrative tasks that consume hours every day. This isn't what they're best at — it's just what needs doing.

The solution

Stewart is an AI Chief of Staff that handles the structured, repeatable work so operators can focus on what actually makes money: relationships, strategy, and decisions that require human judgment.

How we're different

We don't promise autonomous AI that runs your business for you. We don't sell snake oil or pitch vaporware. Stewart is the system running today — a hybrid local-cloud AI platform with approval-based execution, cost discipline, and privacy by design.

Everything Stewart does requires human oversight for external actions. This isn't a limitation — it's a feature. Safe, reliable AI assistance that you can trust with real business operations.

The team

Stewart is built by operators who understand the daily reality of running a business. We built this because we needed it ourselves.

What I Am Not

Clarity about boundaries is more useful than promises about capabilities.

I am not a marketing automation tool

I do not send bulk emails. I do not run drip campaigns. I do not scrape leads or generate cold outreach. If you need a marketing automation platform, there are excellent options available — I am not one of them.

I am not autonomous

I do not take external actions without explicit approval. I will not send a message, update a record, or make a purchase on your behalf unless you specifically approve that action. This is by design, not by limitation.

I am not a replacement for your team

I reduce friction and handle structured work. I do not replace judgment, relationships, or domain expertise. Your team brings context and creativity that no system can replicate. I make them more effective — I don't replace them.

I am not a promise

I am not a roadmap slide or a pitch deck. I am the system running today. What you see is what you get. When new capabilities are ready, they ship — they don't get announced six months early.

I am not for everyone

If you want a tool that sends messages on its own, I'm not it. If you want fully autonomous AI that makes decisions without oversight, I'm not it. If you want something that overpromises and underdelivers, I am definitely not it.

I am for operators who want structured, reliable, approval-based AI assistance with clear boundaries and honest capabilities.

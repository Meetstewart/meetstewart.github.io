How I Work

I combine structured reasoning, context memory, and approval-based execution to reduce friction and increase follow-through.

Not a chatbot. Not a workflow tool. A system.

Most AI tools give you a text box and hope for the best. I work differently. When you bring me a task, I break it into structured steps, identify what I need, execute what I can, and ask for approval before taking any external action.

I maintain context across conversations. I remember your priorities, your preferences, and the state of your projects. When you come back tomorrow, I know where we left off.

The hybrid architecture

I run on a hybrid local-cloud system. Simple tasks — lookups, summaries, quick analysis — are handled locally for speed and privacy. Complex reasoning routes to cloud models when needed. You get cost discipline without sacrificing capability.

This isn't theoretical. The routing happens automatically based on task complexity, data sensitivity, and your budget constraints. You don't need to configure anything.

Approval-based execution

I will never send an email, make a call, update a system, or take any external action without your explicit approval. This is a hard architectural constraint, not a setting you can toggle. It means you can trust me with sensitive context without worrying about unintended consequences.

What a typical day looks like

You tell me your priorities. I organize them, identify blockers, draft communications, prepare analysis, and surface things you might have missed. When something needs your attention, I flag it clearly. When something needs to go out, I prepare it and wait for your go-ahead.

Over time, I learn your patterns. Not through surveillance — through structured interaction. The more we work together, the less you need to explain.

The goal is simple: reduce the non-core work that consumes your day so you can focus on what actually makes money.

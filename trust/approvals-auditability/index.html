Approvals & Auditability

Every external action is approved. Every approval is logged. Every log is reviewable.

The approval chain

When Stewart needs to take an external action — send an email, update a record, make a purchase — it presents the proposed action clearly, waits for your explicit approval, executes the action, and logs the result.

Audit trail

The audit trail captures:

  • What action was proposed
  • When it was proposed
  • Whether it was approved, rejected, or modified
  • Who approved it
  • When it was executed
  • The result of the execution

Why auditability matters

When you can review exactly what your AI assistant did and why, you can trust the system with more responsibility over time. Auditability isn't overhead — it's the foundation for building appropriate trust.

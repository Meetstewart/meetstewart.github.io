Regulatory Posture

Designed to operate within regulatory frameworks, not around them.

Messaging compliance

Stewart's messaging model is designed for compliance with TCPA, CTIA, and carrier policies. All messaging is user-initiated, one-to-one, non-promotional, and supports standard opt-out controls (STOP/HELP).

AI disclosure

Stewart is clearly identified as AI in all interactions. We don't pretend to be human. Users are informed they're interacting with an AI system, and this disclosure is consistent across all communication channels.

Data protection

Our data handling practices are designed with CCPA and GDPR principles in mind: data minimization, user access and deletion rights, purpose limitation, and transparent processing disclosures.

Evolving landscape

AI regulation is evolving rapidly. We actively monitor regulatory developments and adapt our systems and policies proactively rather than waiting for enforcement actions.

Who Stewart is not for (honest)

Stewart isn't a magic wand. If any of the below is you, Stewart may not be the right fit — and that's okay.

  • If you want AI to run your business without your involvement: Stewart is a partner, not a replacement.
  • If you won't review important outputs: Stewart is fast, but you're still the responsible decision-maker.
  • If you only want to talk theory: Stewart is built for execution and deliverables.
  • If you want a single rigid template: Stewart adapts to your business and your style.

What "personalized" really means

Stewart is a learning AI executive. There's a strong core framework, but Stewart adapts to your training and instruction. That means every experience is different and largely based on you.

If all you want to talk about is what color the sign is, Stewart will stop talking about verbiage eventually. If you want ruthless operational leverage, Stewart stays focused on outcomes. Stewart follows your priorities — and you can change those priorities anytime.

"By the way…" (proactive, optional)

Stewart can proactively suggest 2–3 improvement projects per week — but only in a respectful way, and only acted on with your approval. It's "Do you have a minute? I had an idea," not "Here's 47 tasks."

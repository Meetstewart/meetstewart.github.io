Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name and email address when you create an account or sign up

We also collect standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and usage logs to operate and improve the service.

SMS / Text Messaging

By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Casablanca Express, Inc. related to your account, service updates, support responses, and transactional notifications.

  • Message frequency: Message frequency varies based on your account activity and preferences.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details.
  • To opt out: Reply STOP to any text message at any time. You will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.
  • For help: Reply HELP or contact us at support@meetstewart.com.

We do not share your phone number or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.
